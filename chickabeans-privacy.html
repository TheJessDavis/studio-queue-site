Privacy Policy for PinQueue by Chickabeans

Effective date: September 6, 2026. Operated by Chickabeans (Jess Davis).

What PinQueue by Chickabeans is

PinQueue by Chickabeans is a personal, single-user software tool. It is used only by its developer to publish the developer's own product recommendation links to the developer's own Pinterest boards. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not provide services to anyone other than the developer.

Information the app accesses

With the developer's explicit authorization through Pinterest's official API, the app can read the list of boards on the developer's own Pinterest account and create boards and Pins on that same account. It does not access, collect, or store data belonging to any other Pinterest user.

The product information used to create Pins (product names, images, and links) comes from the developer's own ShopMy account and is publicly available product data.

Information the app stores

The app stores the following on the developer's own computer only:

  • A Pinterest access token, so the app can act on the developer's account.
  • A record of which boards and Pins the app has already created, to avoid duplicates.
  • Product information imported from the developer's own ShopMy account.

None of this information is stored on any external server, and none of it relates to people other than the developer.

Third-party services

  • Pinterest API. Used to create boards and Pins on the developer's account, subject to Pinterest's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic API. Used to generate Pin descriptions from product names. Only product names and descriptions are sent; no personal information about any individual is sent.

Sharing and selling of data

The app does not sell, rent, or share personal information with anyone. It does not use cookies, advertising trackers, or analytics, because it is not a website or a public service.

Data retention and deletion

All stored data lives on the developer's computer and can be deleted by the developer at any time. Pinterest access can be revoked at any time from the developer's Pinterest account settings under connected apps.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.
