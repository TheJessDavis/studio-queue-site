Chickabeans

Handmade goods and curated product recommendations by Jess Davis.

Chickabeans is a small creator brand. Jess shares handmade items in the Chickabeans Etsy shop and curated craft, hobby and home product picks in the Chickabeans ShopMy shop. Those picks are also shared as Pins on the Chickabeans Pinterest account.

PinQueue by Chickabeans

PinQueue is a private, single-user desktop tool that Jess built and runs on her own computer to plan and publish Pins to the Chickabeans Pinterest account. It writes the title, description, alt text and keywords for each Pin, saves the Pin to a chosen board, and publishes at a scheduled time. It is not distributed to anyone else and only ever creates Pins on the Chickabeans account.
